I'm Jeya Vardhan, a computer science student at GITAM University in Visakhapatnam, on the east coast of India.

I like problems that come with a clock. A shell has to hand the terminal to the right process at the right moment. A LiDAR scan has to be matched before the next one arrives, 100 milliseconds later. A rocket has to correct itself faster than it can tip over. Most of what I build sits on that line between code and the physical thing it controls.

I learn by building the thing from the bottom up: writing the shell instead of reading about shells, wiring the sensor instead of using a library that hides it. Then I write down what broke, because that is usually the interesting part.

Outside of that I collect pictures, a lot of them. A few are behind the door at the end of this page.
